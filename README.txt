Shield Status (Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.11, client-side only)
Look at a player: green "SHIELD UP" while blocking, red "SHIELD DISABLED 4.2s" after their shield is disabled.
Logs "Shield disabled detected on <name>" to latest.log when it sees a shield-disable.
Tweak duration / logging in ShieldTracker.java (DISABLE_MS, DEBUG_LOG).
